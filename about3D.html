3D Commission Services:

Character Textures:

I will retexture your character model in the style of your character. Cost varies based on character complexity and the type of model.

  • Character Texture: $20-$200 + [15% of Asset Cost]
  • New Character Models: $15 + [100% of Asset Cost]

    • All 3D Character Texturing Services include a complimentary 30 minute photo session.

    Clothing/Accessory retextures

    I will retexture your clothing and accessories. Cost varies based on complexity and the type of model.

  • Clothing/Accessory Texture: $5-$15 + [15% of Asset Cost] per clothing or accessory
  • New Clothing/Accessory Models: $5 + [100% of Asset Cost] per clothing or accessory

    • Unity Setup

    I can do any Unity setup for all platforms: PC, Quest, Android. I will attempt to add any accessories, add-ons, or shaders, as long as they agree with my TOS, I have the rights to use them, and they still work for your targeted platform and character model. I currently cannot do any Blender optimization.

  • Unity Setup: $10-$50 + [Any licensing or asset fees]

    • Unity setup services must comply with the TOS of all assets used. Typically this means that both Moon Maker Studios and the client must own all assets involved in the project. Where necessary, a non-refundable asset or license fee will be added to the cost of the commission.

    All 3D models have an extended rework period. Additional fixes may be requested up to one week after files are sent.
